Hello! Welcome to my homepage!

I am an FDS postdoctoral fellow at the Institute for Foundations of Data Science, Yale University, hosted by Prof. Daniel A. Spielman. Previously, I obtained my Ph.D. degree in probability and mathematical statistics at the School of Mathematical Sciences, Peking University, where I was advised by Prof. Dayue Chen and Prof. Jian Ding. I obtained my B.S. degree in statistics from the Department of Mathematics, Shandong University.

My research interest is probability theory. My Erdős Number is 3.
